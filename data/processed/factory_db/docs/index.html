What this is. A working list of U.S. factories that build residential modular housing, plus candidates still being verified and builders that deploy modular homes. In scope for the manufacturer survey: U.S. factories that build complete volumetric modules for housing and are not HUD-code only. Types: Factory = confirmed factory row; Candidate = possible factory still being verified; Builder = builder/developer, not a factory. Survey: Eligible meets all scope rules; Check needs one more fact confirmed. Contact details, outreach records and notes are kept private and are not published here. Research in progress; figures will change.
