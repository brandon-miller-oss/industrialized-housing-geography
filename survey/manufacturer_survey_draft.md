# Draft Manufacturer Survey: Industrialized Housing Production and Distribution

**Status:** Working draft for discussion with the CHRIS faculty lead. Check whether IRB review is needed before any outreach.

**Purpose:** Collect what public data does not report: where factory-built homes are shipped, who buys them, and how policy affects demand.

**Target respondents:** HUD-Code plants (MHI plant list), state-certified modular and panelized manufacturers (starting with Colorado DOH and neighboring states), and IHIP recipients.

**Confidentiality:** Individual responses are confidential. Results are reported only in aggregates of 3 or more firms.

---

### A. Firm and plant
1. Company name, plant location(s), and parent company (if any)
2. Product types: HUD-Code / modular (volumetric) / panelized / tiny home / other
3. Code basis for each product (HUD-Code, IRC, IBC) and state certifications held
4. Year the plant opened; approximate number of employees

### B. Production and capacity
5. Homes (or modules) produced in the most recent full year
6. Maximum annual capacity at the current staffing level
7. Average share of capacity used over the past 12 months

### C. Distribution (core question)
8. Of last year's homes, about how many went to each state? (table: state × units)
9. For Colorado shipments: which counties or cities? (optional)
10. Typical maximum shipping distance from the plant (miles)

### D. Buyers and end use
11. Share of sales by buyer type: retailers/dealers · land-lease communities · individual owners · for-profit developers · nonprofit/affordable developers · public housing authorities · other
12. Share of units going to income-restricted or subsidized projects (LIHTC, Prop 123, HOME, other)
13. Average wholesale price per home or per square foot (ranges are fine)

### E. Policy and barriers
14. Rate each as a barrier to selling in a jurisdiction (1–5): local zoning · local building code amendments · permitting time · transportation limits · financing/appraisal · NIMBY opposition · foundation/site costs
15. Have state incentives (e.g., Colorado IHIP) changed your production or market? How?
16. Which states or cities are easiest and hardest to sell into, and why? (open-ended)

### F. Follow-up
17. May we contact you for a short interview? (Y/N, contact)
